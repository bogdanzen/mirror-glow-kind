# Oglinda

## Register
product

## Users
Visitors at a shopping mall interacting with a two-metre portrait touchscreen, with a phone available for QR donations.

## Product Purpose
A Romanian cancer-awareness campaign for Vertical Freedom and Lions Club. Visitors see a personal reflection, consider prevention, and can support screening and mental-health access.

## Brand Personality
Calm, dignified and empathetic. Follow the user's supplied Figma compositions rather than generic app layouts.

## Anti-references
No busy dashboard treatment in the campaign, no decorative camera streaming, and no visible AI portrait disclaimer on the main experience.

## Design Principles
Keep the kiosk stable on Android. Preserve the consent flow. Make donation optional and measure anonymous interactions separately from actual payments. Respect the supplied portrait proportions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large legible Romanian text, touch-first controls, no scroll or zoom on the kiosk, automatic inactivity reset, and reduced-motion support for ambient video.
